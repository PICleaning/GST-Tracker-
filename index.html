🔒 Privacy Policy

🛡️ 100% Local — Your data never leaves your device

Smart GST Tracker by Endless Digital World (EDW) is committed to your privacy.

What data is collected?
None. EDW does not collect, store, transmit, or have access to any data you enter into this app. Zero.

Where is my data stored?
All data entered in this app is stored locally in your browser (localStorage) on your device only. It is also shared directly with your own Smart GST Google Sheet via Google Apps Script — a connection you control.

Does EDW see my Google Sheet data?
No. The Google Apps Script runs inside your own Google account. EDW has no access to your sheet, your entries, or your financial information.

Third parties?
This app uses Google Fonts (DM Sans, DM Serif Display) for display purposes only. No analytics, no tracking scripts, no advertising SDKs are included.

Data deletion?
You can delete all locally stored data at any time via Settings → Delete All My Data in the menu.
