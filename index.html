🔒 Privacy Policy

🛡️ 100% Local — Your data never leaves your device

Smart GST Tracker by Endless Digital World (EDW) is committed to your privacy.

What data is collected?
None. EDW does not collect, store, transmit, or have access to any data you enter into this app. Zero.

Where is my data stored?
All data entered in this app is stored locally in your browser (localStorage) on your device only. It is also shared directly with your own Smart GST Google Sheet via Google Apps Script — a connection you control.

Does EDW see my Google Sheet data?
No. The Google Apps Script runs inside your own Google account. EDW has no access to your sheet, your entries, or your financial information.

Third parties?
This app uses Google Fonts (DM Sans, DM Serif Display) for display purposes only. No analytics, no tracking scripts, no advertising SDKs are included.

Data deletion?
You can delete all locally stored data at any time via Settings → Delete All My Data in the menu.

ℹ️ About This App

💰
Smart GST Tracker
by Endless Digital World · Version 3.0.3
📱 Web App Features
💰 Income & Expense Tracking
Record income and expenses with GST automatically split at your chosen rate (default 10%). Log income weekly, monthly, or by category. Supports unlimited expense categories and up to 10 custom income sources per month.
☁️ Google Sheets Sync
Every entry syncs instantly to your own private Google Sheet via Apps Script. Your data stays inside your Google account — Endless Digital World never sees it. Pull existing sheet data back into the app anytime.
🏆 Gamified Progress
Monthly profit rankings with 🥇🥈🥉 medals, interactive bar charts showing income, expenses, profit or GST by month, and performance statistics including streak count and best-ever month.
📂 My Logs
Browse all entries by month and type. Export a detailed PDF report for any period. Manage or delete data securely from here. Filter by income only or expenses only.
🔒 PIN Lock
Set a 4-digit PIN to protect your financial data. Enable it under Settings → Security. The PIN is required every time you open the app.
🗂️ Custom Expense Categories
Add your own expense categories beyond the 9 defaults. Auto-detects the best emoji for your category name. Drag to reorder, delete anytime. New categories sync to your Google Sheet automatically. Access via the Expenses screen.
🔁 Recurring Expenses
Set up subscriptions, memberships, and bills that auto-log each month, quarter, or year. Recurring entries sync to your Google Sheet automatically when processed. Access via ☰ Menu → Recurring Expenses.
🎯 Budget Goals
Set a monthly income target and track your progress with a live progress bar. Budget status shown on the Progress tab and at the top of the hamburger menu for quick access. Access via ☰ Menu → Budget Goals.
📁 My Documents
Attach photo or PDF receipts when logging expenses. All receipts are saved here and viewable or downloadable anytime. Storage is local to your browser. Access via ☰ Menu → My Documents.
🏅 Rewards & Points
Earn points every time you log income — bigger entries earn more. Sheet-imported income also earns points if you haven't logged manually yet. Reach milestones to unlock exclusive EDW rewards: 25,000 pts — early feature access (tag @Edwfinance on Instagram to enrol). Higher milestones available — see Privacy Policy → Terms & Conditions for full details. Access via ☰ Menu → Rewards & Points.
⚙️ Settings
Customise the GST rate (default 10% for Australia), manage your Google Sheet URL, enable or change your PIN lock, and manage your data. Access via ☰ Menu → Settings.
🔔 Push Notification Reminders
Receive in-app alerts when you haven't logged income for a week, plus BAS lodgement date reminders before each quarterly ATO deadline. Enable or disable individually in Settings → Notifications.
📊 Monthly Summary Notifications
At the end of each month, an auto-generated digest shows your total income, expenses, and GST payable for that month — helping you stay on top of your finances and BAS obligations. Toggle in Settings → Notifications.
💤 Inactivity Nudge
If no entries have been added in 7 or more days, the app gently prompts you to log any income or expenses you may have missed. Toggle in Settings → Notifications.
📧 Email Verification
New users verify their email address with a 6-digit one-time code before accessing the app. This links your profile securely to your email for future support.
📜 Terms & Conditions
Earning points may qualify you for exclusive offers available only to eligible members. View full terms via ☰ Menu → Privacy Policy → Terms & Conditions.
🔄 Auto Updates
The app is hosted online and updates automatically every time you open it. No re-download or reinstall needed — new features appear the next time you visit.
🛡️ Privacy & Local Storage
All data is stored locally on your device using localStorage and IndexedDB (up to 7 years of data). Your profile persists even after clearing browser history — just re-enter your name to restore everything. EDW never collects your financial data.
📊 Google Sheet Features
📅 12 Monthly Sheets
One tab per month (January–December). Each sheet tracks income entries, expense categories, GST amounts, and ex-GST totals.
📈 Dashboard Overview
The Dashboard tab shows a full financial year overview — income, expenses, net profit and GST payable for every month at a glance.
📋 Quarterly ATO Breakdown
Quarterly totals aligned to ATO BAS periods (Q1–Q4) with GST payable per quarter — makes BAS lodgement much easier.
⚡ Apps Script Integration
The built-in Apps Script connects the sheet to this app. It handles writing new entries and reading back data for the sync feature.
💡 Tips
• Always enter amounts including GST — the app splits it out automatically.
• Use the Get Data from Sheet button in the Progress tab to sync existing sheet data.
• Bookmark this app in your browser so you can open it instantly anytime.
• Export a PDF from My Logs at the end of each quarter for your records.
• The GST rate can be changed in Settings if it ever changes from 10%.
