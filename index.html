🔒 Privacy Policy

🛡️ 100% Local — Your data never leaves your device

Smart GST Tracker by Endless Digital World (EDW) is committed to your privacy.

What data is collected?
None. EDW does not collect, store, transmit, or have access to any data you enter into this app. Zero.

Where is my data stored?
All data entered in this app is stored locally in your browser (localStorage) on your device only. It is also shared directly with your own Smart GST Google Sheet via Google Apps Script — a connection you control.

Does EDW see my Google Sheet data?
No. The Google Apps Script runs inside your own Google account. EDW has no access to your sheet, your entries, or your financial information.

Third parties?
This app uses Google Fonts (DM Sans, DM Serif Display) for display purposes only. No analytics, no tracking scripts, no advertising SDKs are included.

Data deletion?
You can delete all locally stored data at any time via Settings → Delete All My Data in the menu.

ℹ️ About This App

💰
Smart GST Tracker
by Endless Digital World · Version 1.8.1
📱 Web App Features
💰 Income & Expense Tracking
Record income and expenses with GST automatically split out at 10%. Supports up to 10 income entries per month and unlimited expense categories.
☁️ Google Sheets Sync
Every entry syncs instantly to your own private Google Sheet. Your data stays in your Google account — EDW never sees it.
🏆 Gamified Progress
Monthly profit rankings with 🥇🥈🥉 medals, income vs expense charts, GST payable trends, and performance stats to keep you motivated.
📂 My Logs
Browse all entries by month and type. Export a PDF report for any period. Manage and delete data securely from here.
🔒 PIN Lock
Set a 4-digit PIN to protect your financial data. Enable it under Settings.
🗂️ Custom Categories
Add your own expense categories beyond the defaults. Manage them anytime via Manage Categories in the menu.
🔄 Auto Updates
The app is hosted online. New features and improvements are delivered automatically every time you open it — no re-download needed.
📊 Google Sheet Features
📅 12 Monthly Sheets
One tab per month (January–December). Each sheet tracks income entries, expense categories, GST amounts, and ex-GST totals.
📈 Dashboard Overview
The Dashboard tab shows a full financial year overview — income, expenses, net profit and GST payable for every month at a glance.
📋 Quarterly ATO Breakdown
Quarterly totals aligned to ATO BAS periods (Q1–Q4) with GST payable per quarter — makes BAS lodgement much easier.
⚡ Apps Script Integration
The built-in Apps Script connects the sheet to this app. It handles writing new entries and reading back data for the sync feature.
💡 Tips
• Always enter amounts including GST — the app splits it out automatically.
• Use the Get Data from Sheet button in the Progress tab to sync existing sheet data.
• Bookmark this app in your browser so you can open it instantly anytime.
• Export a PDF from My Logs at the end of each quarter for your records.
• The GST rate can be changed in Settings if it ever changes from 10%.

Using a Private Browser?
